# Market Square governance gates

The August 2026 PRD identifies several unresolved or approval-dependent mechanics. They are configuration gates, not implied launch commitments.

| Capability | Default | Enablement evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VIP access timing | Off | Written entitlement timing, refund and replay rules |
| Live KASH gifts | Off | Approved utility definition, ledger transaction, receipt, reconciliation and support path |
| Paid/earned verification economics | Proposed only | Eligibility, pricing, fairness, duration, revocation and appeal approval |
| Money-linked games | Off | Legal, fairness and age-gating approval |
| Prediction or staking mechanics | Off | Legal, fairness, age-gating and commercial approval |

Public flags are presentation controls only. The platform gateway must independently enforce every entitlement and economic rule.
